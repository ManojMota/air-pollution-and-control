# Air Pollution and Control – Learning Resources

## About This Repository

This repository provides open educational resources for the undergraduate course **Air Pollution and Control**, with a focus on understanding air pollution, its sources, impacts, prevention, control technologies, vehicular emissions, and ambient air quality management.

The resources are intended primarily for undergraduate engineering students, educators, and learners interested in air pollution science and environmental engineering.

## Learning Resources

The repository contains PowerPoint presentations covering the following subject areas:

### 1. Introduction to Air Pollution
- Fundamentals and introduction to air pollution
- Effects of air pollution
- Global environmental issues

### 2. Air Pollution Prevention and Control
- Source control and process changes
- Equipment modification
- Settling chambers
- Cyclone centrifugal separators
- Fabric bag filters
- Wet scrubbers and electrostatic precipitators

### 3. Vehicular Air Pollution
- Traffic as a source of air pollution
- Vehicular pollutants and their detrimental effects
- Mitigation measures for vehicular air pollution
- Traffic management systems for air quality improvement

### 4. Air Quality Modelling
- Introduction to CALROADS VIEW
- Classroom instruction and software familiarisation
- Roadway air pollution modelling

### 5. Ambient Air Quality Monitoring and Standards
- Ambient air quality monitoring
- Continuous Emission Monitoring System (CEMS)
- Air Quality Index (AQI)
- National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)
- Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981

### 6. Emerging Technologies
- Technological advancements in air pollution control

## How to Use These Resources

- Browse the presentation files available in this repository.
- Select the topic relevant to your learning requirements.
- Download the PowerPoint presentations for academic and educational use.
- Use the presentations as supplementary learning material alongside prescribed textbooks and other academic references.
